PRIVACY & FAIRNESS

What the camera checks

Face, eye, hand, and pose landmarks are processed locally to help with framing, visible hands, posture, movement, lighting, focus, and image clarity.

  • No recognition or emotion analysis. The system does not identify or judge your face.
  • No raw camera upload. Only aggregate calibration ratios, feedback, and timestamped setup or interruption events are stored—never images or raw landmarks.
  • No automatic cheating or hiring decision. Visual and browser signals require human context and are separated from the discussion recap.
  • Resume text is treated as untrusted context. It helps the interviewer ask relevant questions.
